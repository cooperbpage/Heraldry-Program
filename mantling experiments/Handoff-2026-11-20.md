## Required design

Continue the replacement architecture in plume/v2. Each strip has a smooth G2 cubic Bézier spine, tapering width profile, and cluster/fold/branch/volute events at normalized arc-length stations. Cluster intervals must leave at least 40 percent of each stem bare. Recursive clusters have 2–4 asymmetric, recurving leaflets and scale with local width. Fold-over narrows the strip, crosses it with a fold edge, and exposes persistent white lining beyond the fold. Default face is red. Children inherit the parent's face and default behind the parent. A volute grows from any station as a tangent-continuous logarithmic spiral of increasing curvature. Shading describes a concave channel, darker groove, highlighted edges, cupped lobes and overlap/helm occlusion. Hatching follows cross-contours.

Only the six requested tools are built. Sweep draw, bracket cluster, fork, pinch width, volute by eye and fold. Compose on draw decorates 1–3 mirrored main sweeps; Reseed changes generated ornament while retaining the authored sweeps. Manual changes preserve generated elements across reseeds. Reset, geometry-only undo/redo, adjustable smoothing, independent colour/outline/midrib/shading/hatching and SVG export are implemented. Garamond Premier Pro Medium Caption fonts are embedded, with long s and italic double-s substitution. Shield is a quarter of the sheet width.
