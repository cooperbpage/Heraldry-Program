# Independent visual review

Nine specimens tested three guide arrangements with three constructions. The independent critic inspected the first plate, compared it with the supplied achievements, and reviewed the revised geometry at full scale and at reduced size.

| Construction | First proof | Revision and judgment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Acanthus | Regular lobe spacing made some edges look saw-toothed. | Lobe spans vary and tight inside bends suppress detail. Volutes with Acanthus is the closest of the nine to the vine-like Saint-Clair reference (IMG_8975.jpeg). |
| Slashed cloth | Some deep cuts resembled spearheads. Several colour changes lacked a visible turning edge. | Curved slits and oblique turnover lips improve the sense of cloth folding back. Canopy with Slashed cloth shows the two surfaces most clearly. |
| Scrollwork | Equal, wire-like subsidiary curls competed with the major sweep. | Varying the weight and frequency of the shoots makes Cascade easier to read at small scale. |

In the first Canopy plate a lower branch appeared detached. The revised guide carries the shoulder out from behind the helmet, places the smaller branch under its parent and preserves the adjacent opening. The critic could trace the revised cloth continuously from helmet to tip.

The broad white gathered folds in IMG_8976–78 remain fuller than the prototype's strips. At phone size tiny turnover lips and hatching become subdued. Exact bilateral symmetry, requested for the experiment, gives a more regular rhythm than the painted references.

The final engine was exercised in all 288 combinations of three compositions, three constructions, and 32 settings of the five rendering switches. Empty, short, duplicate and reversing guide points were also tested. Freehand branching and mirrored drawing were tested in a browser. Physical iPhone Safari was not tested.
